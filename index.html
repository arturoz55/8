FAQ

Questions traders ask.

Is the terminal on this page real?

No. The board, prices and trades above are simulated in your browser with a paper balance of 10 SOL. Nothing connects to a wallet or a blockchain.

Who holds my funds?

You do. Kestrel is designed around a non-custodial wallet whose private key you can export whenever you want.

What does "graduated" mean?

A token graduates when its bonding curve fills and liquidity moves to a regular DEX pool. Pulse shows each token's progress so you can see it coming.

Which chains are supported?

Solana for spot trading. Perpetuals settle on a Solana-native perps venue.
