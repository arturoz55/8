Architecture

Integrations

Apogee routes through the DEXs, launchpads, perps venues and staking pools you already use, all from one account.

Non-custodial

Your wallet key is generated and stored on your side. Apogee can sign only what you approve and can never move funds on its own.

Security

Speed is useless if your funds are not safe. Here is how Apogee protects them.

Your keys, always

Wallet keys are created and stored in a secure enclave tied to your login. Export them whenever you like and take them to any other wallet.

MEV protection

Swaps go out through private relays by default, which keeps sandwich bots from front-running your orders.

Withdrawal delay you control

Turn on a 24-hour delay for new withdrawal addresses. If someone gets into your account, you have a day to stop them.

Token risk checks

Every token shows mint and freeze authority (or contract ownership on Ethereum), top-10 holder share and dev wallet activity before you buy.

Passkeys and 2FA

Log in with a passkey or an authenticator app. SMS codes are never used.

Open about incidents

If something goes wrong, we publish a full write-up within 72 hours, including what we changed.

FAQ

What is Apogee?

Apogee is a trading terminal for 9 chains: Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Avalanche, Optimism and Robinhood Chain. It puts new token launches, DEX pairs, wallet tracking, perps and yield on a single screen, with hotkeys so you can act in one press.

Who holds my funds?

You do. Apogee creates a self-custody wallet for you and never takes control of your keys. You can export the private key at any time and use it in any Solana wallet.

What does "graduated" mean?

Many new tokens start on a bonding curve. When the curve fills, its liquidity moves to a regular DEX pool and the token "graduates". Pulse shows each token's progress so you can see it coming.

How much does it cost?

There is no subscription. Each swap pays a flat fee of 0.75%, and part of that comes back to you as cashback. Higher tiers return more, up to half of your fees.

How do referrals pay?

You earn 30% of the fees paid by traders you invite directly, 3% from the people they invite, and 2% from the level after that. Earnings are paid in SOL.

How fast are orders?

Orders are sent through several relays at once with your chosen priority fee. Most land in the next block: about 400 milliseconds on Solana and about 12 seconds on Ethereum mainnet.

Can I lose more than I put in on perps?

No. Your loss on a position is limited to the margin you put in. If the price reaches your liquidation level, the position closes and the rest of your balance is untouched.

Where does yield come from?

Idle balances are staked with validators on their own networks. Rewards are added automatically, and the rate changes with each chain and with network conditions.

Is there a mobile app?

Native iOS and Android apps are on the way. Today the terminal works in any mobile browser and can be added to your home screen.

Which chains are supported?

Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Avalanche, Optimism and Robinhood Chain. Switch with one click from the hero or the chain menu in the terminal. Each chain keeps its own balance and positions. Connect a Solana wallet, an Ethereum-compatible wallet or both; when you pick a chain, Apogee asks your wallet to switch network.
