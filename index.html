FAQ

What is Kestrel?

A trading terminal for on-chain markets. It brings new launches, wallet tracking, perps and yield onto one screen. The terminal on this page is a simulation with a paper balance.

Who holds my funds?

You do. The wallet is non-custodial and you can export its private key at any time.

What does "graduated" mean?

A token graduates when its bonding curve fills and its liquidity moves to a regular DEX pool.

How do fees and cashback work?

Each swap pays a flat 0.75% fee. Part of it comes back to you as cashback, and higher tiers return more.

Which chains are supported?

Solana for spot trading. Perps settle on a Solana-native venue.
